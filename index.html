about

I'm pursuing dual majors in AI and Decision Making and Business Analytics at MIT. My interests are at the intersection of machine learning and operations research — using models to forecast likely scenarios, then optimization to make critical decisions.

That framework has taken me across medical technology with Presurgeo, climate science with the BC3 project, public health logistics, and government-facing security systems. I'm also commissioning into the United States Space Force through Air Force ROTC, which has shaped an interest in building for mission-driven, resource-constrained environments.
